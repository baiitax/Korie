## 🏛️ Executive Brand & Architecture Overview

KoriePay is a Tier-1 financial technology infrastructure platform engineered to connect physical agents, foreign exchange (BDC) operators, consumers, merchants, and corporate enterprises across West Africa.

### 🌟 Three Primary Ecosystem Pillars
1. **AGENCY BANKING:** Last-mile financial access, cash-in, cash-out, smart POS terminals, and real-time commission settlement.
2. **BDC / FX DIGITAL:** Real-time rate management, multi-currency treasury virtual accounts (NGN ₦ & XOF CFA), automated compliance logging, and bilateral cross-border liquidity rails.
3. **CUSTOMER WALLETS:** Instant domestic bank transfers, cross-border remittance, dynamic merchant QR checkout, and zero-fee utility vending.
