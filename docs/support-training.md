# Junior Support Officer Training & Sandbox Curriculum

## Fast-Track Onboarding Philosophy
The KoriePay Training Academy enables new junior support hires to become safe, productive frontline operators within **72 hours** through structured simulation modules and sandbox testing:

1. **Module 1: Fintech Fundamentals & Dual-Node Rails**: Understanding how Providus Bank (Nigeria) and Coris Bank (Niger) operate virtual NUBANs, NIP settlement, and XOF wallet clearing.
2. **Module 2: Transaction Troubleshooting & Error Codes**: Reading NIBSS response codes (00, 09, 91, 96), Interswitch ATM error messages, and auto-reversal timers.
3. **Module 3: Frontline Fraud Defense & Social Engineering**: Detecting impersonation, SIM-swap attacks, and data leakage risks.
4. **Interactive Sandbox Simulator**: Practice handling 20 mock customer scenarios in a risk-free environment before production queue activation.
