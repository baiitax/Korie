# Sovereign Capital Management & Regulatory Solvency

## 1. Multi-Jurisdictional Capital Framework
KoriePay maintains sovereign capital tracking aligned with Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and BCEAO regulatory requirements:
- **Eligible Capital Components**: Paid-up Share Capital, Statutory Reserves, Retained Earnings, Current-Period Retained Profit.
- **Capital Adequacy Metrics**: Capital Headroom over statutory minimum license requirements (e.g. ₦2,000,000,000 CBN National PSP requirement, 3,000,000,000 XOF BCEAO E-Money Issuer capital requirement).

---

## 2. Capital Planning & Growth Projections
Forecasts capital positions under multi-year expansion plans (`BASE`, `HIGH_GROWTH`, `EXPANSION_STRESS`), identifying required capital injections before statutory minimum thresholds are breached.
