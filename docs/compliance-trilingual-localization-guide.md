# Trilingual Localization Guide: English, Hausa & French

## Cross-Border Linguistic Architecture
To support regional regulatory officers and compliance analysts seamlessly across Nigeria and Niger Republic, the Compliance Portal is fully localized in **English (EN)**, **Hausa (HA)**, and **French (FR)**.

## Core Financial Crime Terminology Mapping
| English (EN) | Hausa (HA) | French (FR) |
|---|---|---|
| Compliance Command Center | Cibiyar Kula da Ka'idojin Kudi da Tsaro | Centre de Contrôle de la Conformité |
| AML / Anti-Money Laundering | Kula da Zambar Kudi (AML) | Lutte Contre le Blanchiment (LCB) |
| Sanctions Screening | Binciken Jerin Takunkumi | Filtrage des Listes de Sanctions |
| Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) | Rahoton Hada-hadar da Ake Zargi | Déclaration d'Opération Suspecte |
| Customer KYC Verification | Tabbatar da Shaidar Abokan Ciniki | Vérification d'Identité Client (KYC) |
| Account Freeze / Restriction | Sanya Takunkumi / Tsayar da Asusu | Gel des Avoirs / Restriction de Compte |
| Politically Exposed Person (PEP) | Masu Mukamin Gwamnati (PEP) | Personne Exposée Politiquement (PPE) |
