# High-Risk Merchant Monitoring & Settlement Controls

## High-Risk Merchant Categories
- Digital Asset Service Providers (Crypto/Web3)
- Cross-Border Commodity & Grain Arbitrageurs
- FX Bureaux & Remittance Aggregators
- High-Ticket Electronics & Auto Importers

## Settlement Risk Controls
1. **Dynamic Rolling Reserve**: Retention of 5% - 15% of daily settlement volume for 30 to 90 days based on assessed risk grade.
2. **Settlement Delay Tiers (T+1 to T+3)**: Automatic extension of payout settlement windows during investigation of spike anomalies.
3. **Automated Payout Suspension**: Ledger-level freeze of settlement disbursals when chargeback or fraud rates exceed 1.0% volume threshold.
