# Enterprise Policy Governance & Staff Attestations

## 1. Centralized Policy Inventory
KoriePay maintains versioned institutional policies:
- Enterprise Risk Policy
- Group Liquidity & Funding Policy
- Information Security & Cyber Defense Policy
- Anti-Money Laundering & Counter-Terrorist Financing Policy
- Consumer Protection & Redress Policy
- Third-Party Risk & Procurement Policy

---

## 2. Annual Policy Attestation & Compliance Audits
- **Attestation Tracking**: Mandatory employee digital attestation and comprehension verification within 30 days of policy publication.
- **Regulatory Change Management**: Continuous tracking of Central Bank circulars and automated impact assessment workflows.
