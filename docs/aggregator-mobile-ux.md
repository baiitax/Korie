# KORIEPAY AGGREGATOR MOBILE UX & ACCESSIBILITY
## Responsive Breakpoints, Touch Targets & Field Usability

---

## 1. Breakpoints & Viewport Testing

The portal is designed and tested across all viewports:
- **Small Mobile:** 320px – 375px (Compact cards, summary chips, bottom navigation).
- **Standard Mobile:** 390px – 430px (Fluid layouts, 48px+ touch targets).
- **Tablet / iPad:** 768px – 1024px (Adaptive 2-column grids and slide-over drawers).
- **Desktop & Large Displays:** 1280px – 1920px (Full multi-column tables and command centers).

---

## 2. Mobile Bottom Navigation (Field Aggregator Mode)

For field operators on mobile devices:
```
[Home] ──► [Agents] ──► [Float] ──► [Ledger] ──► [More]
```
- **Minimum Touch Target:** 48px × 48px.
- **Offline Safe Shell:** Indicates network drops without showing false successful financial operations.
- **Glassmorphic Contrast:** Meets WCAG 2.2 AA standards with high contrast text on dark slate surfaces.
