# Jurisdictional Regulatory Frameworks: Nigeria (CBN/NFIU) & Niger (BCEAO/CENTIF)

## 1. Federal Republic of Nigeria 🇳🇬
- **Statutory Regulators**: Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU).
- **Governing Legislation**: Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022, Terrorism (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022, CBN AML/CFT/CPF Regulations 2022.
- **Reporting Thresholds**:
  - Cash Transaction Reports (CTR): Individual > ₦5,000,000; Corporate > ₦10,000,000.
  - Suspicious Transaction Reports (STR): Mandatory within 24 hours of suspicion confirmation.
- **Identity Verification Infrastructure**: National Identification Number (NIN via NIMC API), Bank Verification Number (BVN via NIBSS), Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC).

## 2. Republic of Niger (BCEAO / UMOA / CENTIF) 🇳🇪
- **Statutory Regulators**: Banque Centrale des États de l'Afrique de l'Ouest (BCEAO) and Cellule Nationale de Traitement des Informations Financières (CENTIF Niger).
- **Governing Legislation**: Loi Uniforme N° 2016-33 relative à la lutte contre le blanchiment de capitaux et le financement du terrorisme (UMOA zone).
- **Reporting Thresholds**:
  - Cash Transactions > 5,000,000 XOF (CFA).
  - Déclaration de Soupçon (STR): Mandatory filing to CENTIF Niamey within statutory SLA.
- **Identity Verification Infrastructure**: Registre du Commerce et du Crédit Mobilier (RCCM), Numéro d'Identification Fiscale (NIF/TIN), National Passport / Carte Nationale d'Identité.
