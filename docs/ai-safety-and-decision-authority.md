# AI Safety Boundary & Decision Authority Hierarchy

## 1. Non-Bypassable Decision Authority Hierarchy

```
[AI Analytical Model / Copilot]
       │
       ▼ (Generates Structured Output)
[Recommendation + Evidence + Confidence]
       │
       ▼ (Automated Compliance & Policy Filter)
[Risk & Regulatory Validation]
       │
       ▼ (Multi-Tier Human Review)
[Authorized Human Decision Maker (CFO / CRO / COO)]
       │
       ▼ (Dual-Authorization Maker-Checker)
[Authoritative Operational Control Plane]
       │
       ▼ (Permanent Immutable Record)
[Audit Vault]
```

---

## 2. Decision Materiality Tiers

| Tier Level | Name | Scope & Threshold | Required Authority | AI Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Informational | General KPI explanations, trend summaries, FAQ navigation. | Single User | Direct Response |
| **Tier 2** | Operational Recommendation | Low-value customer outreach, feature recommendations. | Functional Analyst | Recommends with Evidence |
| **Tier 3** | Material Business Action | Routing adjustments, agent float rebalancing, campaign budgets. | Head of Department / VP | Formal Approval Required |
| **Tier 4** | High-Impact Enterprise Decision | Liquidity injections, policy modifications, credit limits. | C-Suite Executive (Dual Auth) | Advisory Only / Simulation |

---

## 3. Explicit Prohibited AI Actions

Under no circumstances may AI:
1. Mutate balances or generate debit/credit entries.
2. Override AML suspicious activity holds or sanctions blocks.
3. Automatically alter correspondent bank settlement channels.
4. Modify risk appetite limits or regulatory submission files.
5. Grant or escalate IAM/PAM privileged credentials.
