# Financial Crime Risk Analytics & Typologies Intelligence

## Key Analytical Metrics
1. **Case Conversion Rate**: Ratio of automated AML triggers that result in formal investigation cases and STR disclosures.
2. **Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR)**: Average officer response SLA from initial alert trigger to final decision ruling.
3. **Watchlist Precision & False Positive Rate**: Efficacy of fuzzy match rules across Hausa, French, and English naming variations.
4. **Corridor Risk Exposure**: Real-time cross-border volume aggregation between Lagos, Kano, Maradi, and Niamey.

## Flagged Regional Typologies
- **Cross-Border FX Cash Aggregation**: Large cash deposits collected in peripheral border markets transferred to central trade nodes.
- **Identity Cloning / Proxy Accounts**: Use of stolen or fabricated NIN documents for Tier-2 digital onboarding.
- **Round-Tripping Arbitrage**: Rapid sequential transfers between NGN and XOF wallets to exploit informal market rate differentials.
