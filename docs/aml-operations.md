# AML Operational Workflows & Daily Triaging

## 1. Daily Operational Checklist
1. Monitor P0 Critical Alert Queue (Response SLA $\le 2$ hours).
2. Triage newly generated alert clusters across Nigeria and Niger corridors.
3. Review anomalous expected-activity spikes ($> 300\%$ variance).
4. Conduct 4-eyes review on pending account restriction requests.
5. Verify STR/SAR draft filings before sovereign portal submission.
