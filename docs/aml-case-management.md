# Financial Crime Case Management & Investigation Workbench

## 1. Case Lifecycle & Decisioning
```
OPEN ──> TRIAGE ──> INVESTIGATION ──> INFORMATION_REQUESTED ──> DECISION_PENDING ──> CLOSED
                                                                       │
           REGULATORY_STR_FILING <── ACCOUNT_RESTRICTION <── ENHANCED_MONITORING
```

---

## 2. Investigation Workbench Dimensions
1. **Financial Timeline**: Visual breakdown of deposits, transfers, withdrawals, and FX.
2. **Entity Graph**: 1-hop, 2-hop, and 3-hop relationship clusters.
3. **Immutable Evidence Dossier**: PDF exports, KYC verifications, device fingerprints, and investigator timestamps.
4. **Maker-Checker Authorizations**: High-risk restrictions and STR recommendations require dual compliance sign-off.
