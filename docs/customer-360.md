# Customer 360 & Unified Audit Timeline

## 1. Multi-Dimensional Customer 360 View
The Customer 360 interface synthesizes authoritative data across the entire fintech stack:
- **Identity & KYC Master**: Master Identity ID, NIN/BVN token, verified address.
- **Accounts & Multi-Currency Balances**: Balances sourced directly from Core General Ledger subledgers.
- **Enrolled Products & Limits**: Active banking products, current daily consumption vs limit caps.
- **Registered Devices & Trust**: Device trust scores, hardware models, attestation statuses.
- **Beneficiaries & Transactions**: Full transaction histories, transfer destinations.
- **Risk & AML Cases**: Active flags, PEP/Sanctions reports, open investigations.
- **Consumer Protection**: Active complaints, dispute resolutions, compensation records.
- **Event Timeline**: Chronological event log with timestamp, actor, and correlation ID.
