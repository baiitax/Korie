# Adashi UX Journeys, Persona Workspaces & User Experience

## 1. Customer Adashi Experience

- **Active Rotations Overview**: Visual progress bar showing group cycle progress, total pool value, personal contribution history, and next scheduled debit date.
- **My Rotation Position**: Cryptographically certified slot badge with estimated payout date and projected net payout after platform fees.
- **Consent & Mandate Management**: Electronic mandate authorization with single-click revoke/pause safeguards and debit reminder notifications.
- **Historical Statements**: Downloadable zero-balance audit reports for completed circles.

---

## 2. Agent Adashi Command Center

- **Adashi Group Creation Wizard**: Guided 4-step creation (Product selection, currency/cadence setup, member invites, fee policy).
- **Member Invite & Onboarding Hub**: Dynamic QR codes, shareable WhatsApp/SMS links, and real-time consent tracking.
- **Membership Lock Action**: One-click lock verification with automated pre-flight KYC and balance sanity checks.
- **Cycle & Payout Monitor**: Live collection rate tracker, manual follow-up dispatch, and payout execution trigger.

---

## 3. Super Admin Command Center

- **Platform-Wide Overview**: Cross-jurisdiction Adashi KPI telemetry (Active groups, Total Escrow Vault Balance NGN/XOF, Default Rate, System Compliance Health).
- **Product Factory Manager**: Create, version, and sunset Adashi product templates.
- **Dual-Control Authorization Queue**: Maker-checker review dashboard for high-value payouts, manual rotation overrides, and default write-offs.
- **Compliance & Risk Investigator**: Graph-based syndicate detection, AML flag queue, and escrow reconciliation validator.
