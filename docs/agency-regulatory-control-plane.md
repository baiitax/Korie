# Sovereign Regulatory Control Plane & Compliance Adapters

## 1. Multi-Jurisdictional Regulatory Profiles
Agency banking operations adapt dynamically to sovereign regulatory frameworks:
- **Nigeria (CBN Guidelines for Agency Banking & POS Operations)**:
  - Daily Tier-1 Agent Cash Holding Limits.
  - Mandatory NIN/BVN Verification on Customer Account Opening.
  - Customer Dispute Resolution within statutory CBN deadlines.
- **Niger Republic (BCEAO Financial Inclusion & Mobile Money Directives)**:
  - UEMOA Cross-Border Agent Remittance Reporting.
  - CENTIF Suspicious Cash Movement Declarations.

## 2. Regulatory Evidence Logging
Every transaction authorization decision logs: `(policy_id, version, jurisdiction, rule_code, decision, timestamp)`. This provides verifiable proof for central bank examinations.
