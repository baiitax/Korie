# Recovery Integration with Core Ledger & Reconciliation

## 1. Accounting Posting Invariants
All financial effects generated by the Recovery Engine must pass through `GeneralLedgerEngine.postJournal()`:
- No balance mutation can bypass the double-entry validation rule ($\sum \text{Debits} = \sum \text{Credits}$).
- Every reversal and refund references the `original_transaction_reference`.

---

## 2. Reconciling Recovery Operations
Reversals and refunds generate compensating line items in daily settlement batches. The Reconciliation Engine automatically correlates:
$$\text{Net Settlement} = \text{Gross Inflows} - \text{Successful Outflows} - \text{Refunds} - \text{Reversals} - \text{Chargebacks}$$
This ensures zero orphaned external bank movements at Providus Bank NG and Coris Bank NE.
