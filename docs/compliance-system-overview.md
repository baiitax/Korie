# KoriePay Compliance & Financial Crime Operating System Overview

## Executive Summary
The **KoriePay Compliance & Financial Crime Management Portal** (`/compliance`) serves as the mission-critical regulatory, AML/CFT, due diligence, and risk governance command center across the Federal Republic of Nigeria 🇳🇬 and the Republic of Niger 🇳🇪.

Operating in full compliance with the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), the Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit (NFIU), the Banque Centrale des États de l'Afrique de l'Ouest (BCEAO), and the Cellule Nationale de Traitement des Informations Financières (CENTIF) of Niger, the platform guarantees zero-tolerance financial crime prevention while enabling frictionless legitimate cross-border commerce.

---

## Key Core Capabilities
1. **Compliance Command Hero & Operational Work Queue**: Real-time triage of pending cases, alerts, and verification files.
2. **End-to-End Case Investigation Management**: Full case lifecycle from triage to MLRO signoff and STR filing.
3. **Multi-Tier KYC & KYB Due Diligence Desk**: Automated NIN/BVN biometric matching and CAC/RCCM validation.
4. **Automated AML & Velocity Surveillance**: Real-time detection of smurfing, layering, and sudden velocity spikes.
5. **Sanctions & Watchlist Screening Desk**: Fuzzy match scoring against UN, OFAC, EU, CBN, and BCEAO watchlists.
6. **Maker-Checker Enforcement & Account Freezes**: Cryptographic dual-control authorizations for asset freezes and limits.
7. **Statutory Regulatory Reporting**: Automated generation and dispatch of NFIU CTR/STR and BCEAO CENTIF declarations.
8. **Trilingual Operations (English, Hausa, French)**: Native interfaces and financial crime glossaries.
