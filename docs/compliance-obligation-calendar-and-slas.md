# Compliance Obligation Calendar & SLA Management

## Statutory Deadlines Matrix
| Regulatory Obligation | Jurisdiction | Regulator | Due Frequency | Statutory SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly Cash Transaction Return (CTR) | Nigeria 🇳🇬 | NFIU | Monthly (15th) | Strict Cutoff |
| Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) | Nigeria 🇳🇬 | NFIU | Ad-hoc | <24 hours of suspicion |
| Déclaration de Soupçon (STR) | Niger 🇳🇪 | CENTIF Niger | Ad-hoc | <48 hours of suspicion |
| Quarterly AML Risk Evaluation | Niger 🇳🇪 | BCEAO | Quarterly | End of quarter +15d |
| Annual External AML/CFT Audit | Cross-Border 🌍 | CBN / BCEAO | Annual | Dec 31st |

## Incident Escalation & Overdue Alerting
The system issues automatic notifications when an obligation enters within 72 hours of statutory cutoff, escalating to the Board Audit & Risk Committee if an item becomes overdue.
