# Enterprise Risk Appetite Framework & Sovereign Limits

## 1. Risk Appetite Hierarchy & Threshold Cascades
KoriePay's Risk Appetite Framework (RAF) defines explicit quantitative and qualitative boundaries across all business lines:

$$\text{Risk Appetite} \longrightarrow \text{Risk Tolerance} \longrightarrow \text{Early Warning Threshold} \longrightarrow \text{Hard Limit Breach} \longrightarrow \text{Critical Escalation}$$

### Core Appetite Statement Dimensions:
- **Capital Adequacy & Solvency**: Zero tolerance for capital ratio falling below statutory minimums (CBN National PSP ₦2B, BCEAO E-Money 3B XOF).
- **Liquidity Solvency**: Target liquidity buffer $\ge 150\%$ of 30-day stressed operational requirement.
- **Fraud & Financial Crime**: Net fraud loss capped at $< 0.015\%$ ($1.5\text{ bps}$) of gross transaction volume (GTV).
- **System Availability & Resilience**: High-availability target of $\ge 99.95\%$ for core payment switch and API gateway.
- **Consumer Harm & Redress**: $100\%$ of P0 cash dispense grievances resolved within 24 hours.
