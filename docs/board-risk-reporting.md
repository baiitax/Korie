# Board Risk Reporting & Executive CRO Telemetry

## 1. Board Risk Pack Structure
The Board Risk Committee receives an executive-level risk telemetry pack:
1. **Enterprise Risk Heatmap**: $5 \times 5$ Likelihood vs Impact distribution of Top 10 risks.
2. **Appetite Breaches & Early Warnings**: Real-time status of capital, liquidity, fraud, and cyber thresholds.
3. **Operational Losses & Recoveries**: Gross loss vs insurance and chargeback recoveries.
4. **Key Control Failures & Overdue Remediations**: Issues overdue $> 30\text{ days}$ with executive owner escalation.
5. **Emerging & Horizon Risks**: Regional geopolitical developments, regulatory shifts, and emerging threat vectors.
