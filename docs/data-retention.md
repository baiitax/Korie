# KORIEPAY DATA RETENTION & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE POLICY

## 1. Statutory Retention Periods

| Record Domain | Mandatory Retention | Regulatory Authority | Action at Expiry |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Double-Entry Ledger Postings** | **10 Years** | CBN & BCEAO Banking Acts | Permanent Cold Archive |
| **Transaction History** | **7 Years** | NDPR & NFIU AML Guidelines | Compressed Cold Vault |
| **KYC / Identity Documents** | **7 Years** post account close | CENTIF & WAEMU Directives | Cryptographic Purge |
| **API Request & Webhook Logs** | **90 Days** (Hot), **1 Year** (Cold) | KoriePay Security Policy | Auto-Partition Drop |
| **Audit Events** | **10 Years** | ISO 27001 & SOC 2 Type II | Immutable WORM Storage |
