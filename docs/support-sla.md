# Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Escalation Policies

## Statutory SLA Standards
| Severity | First Response Target | Full Resolution SLA | Auto-Escalation Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CRITICAL** | ≤ 5 minutes | ≤ 30 minutes | 15 minutes post breach |
| **URGENT** | ≤ 15 minutes | ≤ 2 hours | 30 minutes post breach |
| **HIGH** | ≤ 30 minutes | ≤ 4 hours | 1 hour post breach |
| **NORMAL** | ≤ 2 hours | ≤ 24 hours | 4 hours post breach |
| **LOW** | ≤ 8 hours | ≤ 72 hours | 12 hours post breach |

## SLA Breach Alerting & Auto-Reassignment
Tickets entering within 25% of their resolution SLA window receive visual yellow warnings on the officer's dashboard. If a ticket exceeds 100% of its SLA without officer response, the workload engine automatically flags the supervisor and offers re-routing to available team members.
