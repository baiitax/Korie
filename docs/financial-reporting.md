# Financial Reporting & Analytical Engine

## 1. Supported Financial Reports
1. **Trial Balance**: Validates that cumulative debit balances equal cumulative credit balances across all active ledger accounts.
2. **Income Statement (Profit & Loss)**: Displays Revenue (`4000-4999`) less Expenses (`5000-5999`), broken down by jurisdiction and product line.
3. **Balance Sheet**: Displays Assets (`1000-1999`) vs. Liabilities (`2000-2999`) and Equity (`3000-3999`).
4. **Statement of Cash Flows**: Direct cash movement across operating banking channels and liquidity pools.

---

## 2. Multi-Dimensional Drill-Down
Reports can be filtered dynamically by:
- Legal Entity (`Korie Nigeria Ltd` vs. `Korie Niger SA`)
- Product Line (`Merchant Checkout`, `P2P`, `Remittance`)
- Currency (`NGN`, `XOF`, `USD`)
- Time Range (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarter, Fiscal Year)
