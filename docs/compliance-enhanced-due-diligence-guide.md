# Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) & Source of Funds Verification Guide

## Mandatory Triggers for Enhanced Due Diligence
1. Natural or legal persons identified as Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) or PEP associates.
2. Merchants operating in high-risk sectors: Cross-Border FX Bureaux, Virtual Asset Service Providers (VASPs), Precious Metals & Commodity Trading, Non-Profit Organizations (NGOs).
3. Accounts exhibiting cumulative monthly turnover exceeding ₦50,000,000 (or 50,000,000 XOF).
4. Entities located in or transacting with jurisdictions on FATF Grey or Black lists.

## EDD Dossier Requirements
- Certified 6-Month Audited Bank Statements across all operating banks.
- Tax Clearance Certificates (FIRS Nigeria / DGI Niger).
- Documented Source of Wealth narrative with verifiable contracts, bills of lading, and corporate ownership charts.
- Annual mandatory re-certification schedule tracked in the Compliance Obligation Calendar.
